Learn Step by Step: From Zero to Pro

Start from the very beginning or pick up where you are. Every level teaches the English you actually need at work — not random general English.

  1. A1

    Start

    Your first words for work — even if you’ve never studied English.

    Greetings · numbers and prices · property types · rooms

  2. A2

    Basic

    Handle simple conversations and messages with clients.

    Arranging viewings · describing the area · WhatsApp messages

  3. B1

    Confident

    Run a property viewing on your own.

    Showing a property · answering questions · client emails

  4. B2

    Professional

    Negotiate and explain the details of a deal.

    Prices and offers · objections · mortgages · contract terms

  5. C1

    Pro

    Work freely on the international market.

    Presentations · complex negotiations · investment and legal topics

English for Everyday Real Estate Situations

Tap a situation to see phrases you can use today.

🏡Describing a PropertyA1–B1 Features, size and condition
  • “It’s a bright two-bedroom apartment with a sea view.”
  • “The property has been fully renovated.”
📅Arranging a ViewingA2 Dates, times and meeting points
  • “When would be a good time for you to see the property?”
  • “Shall we meet at the main entrance at 3 pm?”
🚪Showing a PropertyB1 Guiding clients room by room
  • “Let me show you the master bedroom.”
  • “As you can see, there’s plenty of storage space.”
❓Answering Client QuestionsB1 Clear, confident replies
  • “That’s a great question — let me check and get back to you today.”
  • “The monthly maintenance fee is around 200 euros.”
🤝Discussing Prices and OffersB2 Negotiation basics
  • “The asking price is 350,000 euros.”
  • “I’ll pass your offer on to the seller.”
✉️Writing Emails and Follow-UpsA2–B2 Professional messages
  • “Thank you for viewing the property today.”
  • “Please let me know if you have any further questions.”

Real Estate English in Practice

A phrase, what it means, and when to use it.

A2

Phrase

“Would you like to arrange a viewing?”

Meaning

Would you like to schedule a visit to see the property?

When to use it

Use this question to invite a client to see a property.

B1

Phrase

“The property is within walking distance of the beach.”

Meaning

You can easily walk from the property to the beach.

When to use it

Use it to highlight a convenient location when describing a property.
