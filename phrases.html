English Phrases for Real Estate Agents

Ready-to-use phrases for real situations with clients, from your first viewing to negotiating an offer. Listen, repeat and use them today.

🏡 Describing a Property📅 Arranging a Viewing🚪 Showing a Property❓ Answering Client Questions🤝 Discussing Prices and Offers✉️ Writing Emails and Follow-Ups

🏡Describing a Property

  • A1

    “It’s a bright two-bedroom apartment.”

    Basic description: size and light.

  • A2

    “The apartment has a large balcony with a sea view.”

    Highlight a key feature.

  • B1

    “The property has been fully renovated.”

    Explain the condition.

  • B1

    “It’s ideal for a family looking for space and privacy.”

    Match the property to the client.

  • B2

    “What makes this property special is its location.”

    Focus on the main selling point.

📅Arranging a Viewing

  • A1

    “When are you free?”

    A short, simple question.

  • A2

    “Would you like to see the apartment on Monday?”

    Suggest a day.

  • A2

    “Shall we meet at the main entrance at 3 pm?”

    Agree on a place and time.

  • B1

    “I’ll send you the address and a location pin.”

    Confirm details by message.

  • B1

    “Could we move the viewing to a slightly later time?”

    Reschedule politely.

🚪Showing a Property

  • A1

    “This is the living room.”

    Introduce each room.

  • A2

    “Let me show you the master bedroom.”

    Guide the client.

  • B1

    “As you can see, there’s plenty of storage space.”

    Point out a benefit.

  • B1

    “Take your time and have a look around.”

    Give the client space.

  • B2

    “Can you imagine yourself living here?”

    Help the client picture life in the home.

❓Answering Client Questions

  • A1

    “Yes, parking is included.”

    A simple, clear answer.

  • A2

    “The monthly service charge is about 200 euros.”

    Give a number clearly.

  • B1

    “That’s a great question — let me check and get back to you today.”

    When you don’t know the answer yet.

  • B1

    “The building was completed in 2021.”

    Give background facts.

  • B2

    “To be completely transparent, there is some construction nearby.”

    Share a disadvantage honestly.

🤝Discussing Prices and Offers

  • A1

    “The price is 350,000 euros.”

    State the price.

  • A2

    “Is the price negotiable?”

    A question clients often ask — learn to recognise it.

  • B1

    “I’ll pass your offer on to the seller.”

    Confirm you will present the offer.

  • B2

    “The seller is open to reasonable offers.”

    Signal room for negotiation.

  • C1

    “I’m afraid the seller has declined, but there may be room for a revised offer.”

    Soften bad news and keep the deal alive.

✉️Writing Emails and Follow-Ups

  • A1

    “Thank you for your message.”

    A polite opening.

  • B1

    “Please find attached the floor plan.”

    Send documents professionally.

  • B1

    “Thank you for viewing the property today.”

    Follow up after a viewing.

  • B1

    “Please let me know if you have any further questions.”

    A friendly closing line.

  • B2

    “I wanted to follow up on our conversation from last week.”

    Restart a conversation politely.
