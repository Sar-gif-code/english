We process your form data based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box in the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After that, we delete it within 30 days.

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or stop sending you emails. Write to integriinfo@gmail.com. Every email we send also has an unsubscribe link.

Cookies

Google Analytics cookies help us count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

Changes
